AI search optimization services (GEO and AEO)

We help businesses show up when customers ask AI or Google where to go or who to call.

What is AI search optimization?

You'll hear it called AI SEO, GEO (generative engine optimization) or AEO (answer engine optimization). They're newer names for the same goal: making sure ChatGPT, Google and the other AI tools can find your business, trust what they read about it, and name it when customers ask. Most of it is the basics done right: your Google profile, reviews, listings, website and business code. The difference is that we check the AI answers themselves every month and show you the screenshots.

How it works

  1. The Check. We ask ChatGPT, Google, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers ask. You get screenshots of every answer.
  2. The Fix. We fix the basics those tools read about your business: your Google profile, your listings and your website.
  3. Every month. We ask the same questions again and send you the before and after. You see exactly what changed.

What we fix

  • Your Google Business Profile: category, services, service area, hours, photos
  • A steady flow of new Google reviews
  • Your Bing Places listing
  • Your Apple Maps listing
  • A website that says what you do, where, and your phone number
  • Making sure search and AI crawlers aren't blocked from your site
  • Business info code on your site that search engines can read
  • The same name, address and phone everywhere you're listed
  • Getting mentioned on other local pages

What you can count on

Our goal is to get your business named first when customers ask AI. We put our full effort behind it every month, and we show you the proof in screenshots.

AI tools decide their own answers, so no honest company can guarantee a spot. What you can count on is the work and the proof, and you'll see whether it's working before you keep paying for it.
